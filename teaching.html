International courses & lectures

  • Chile — Organized and taught a USAID-funded course on Disaster Risk Management, including climate change and nonstationarity, Valdivia (2023).
  • Colombia — USAID-funded course on Disaster Risk Management (2022); two short courses on hydrologic modeling, National University, Medellín.
  • Sri Lanka — Workshop on watershed and river flow modeling with HEC software for 43 engineers (2017); short course on hydrologic risk of extreme events at the EWRI International Perspective on Water Resources conference (2016); climate change talks (2011); short courses for the Sri Lanka Land Reclamation and Development Corporation (SLLRDC); seminars at the Irrigation Department and IWMI; invited lecture, Colombo (2004).
  • Bolivia — Short course on hydrologic risk of extreme events, Tarija and Sucre (2016).
  • Peru — Short course on risk of extreme events under nonstationary conditions, National University of Engineering, Lima (2013).
  • Korea — Statistical modeling of hydrologic extremes, Yonsei University, Seoul (2013); invited lecture on climate and water management (2008).
  • India — U.S. team member, workshop on climate information in water resources management, Pune (2006).
  • Dominican Republic — Principal investigator for hydrologic studies of the Valdesia Reservoir (World Bank); training classes in hydrologic modeling.
  • Spain — Short course on stochastic hydrologic modeling, University of Valencia.
  • Venezuela — Invited paper, Inter-American Conference on Environmental Issues, Caracas (1997).
  • Brazil — Proposal for water management in drought-prone regions of the North and East.
  • United States — ASCE-EWRI short courses on quantifying hydrologic risk of extreme events in a changing climate (Cincinnati, 2013; West Palm Beach, 2016).
