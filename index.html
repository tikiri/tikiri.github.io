About

I work on water resources under a changing climate: rising seas, compound flooding and planning for extremes when the past is no longer a reliable guide to the future.

Jayantha Obeysekera is Director of the Sea Level Solutions Center in the Institute of Environment at Florida International University (FIU), and a Research Professor in FIU’s Department of Earth & Environment and the Extreme Events Institute. The Center works with local and regional governments, agencies and communities in South Florida to turn sea level and climate science into practical adaptation decisions.

Before joining FIU in 2018, he spent more than three decades at the South Florida Water Management District (SFWMD). As Director of the Hydrologic & Environmental Systems Modeling Department and later as Chief Modeler, he led the modeling behind the Kissimmee River and Everglades restoration programs, built a multidisciplinary group of more than 60 modelers spanning hydrology, hydrodynamics, water quality and ecology, and served as the District’s technical lead on climate change and sea level rise.

Nationally, he served on the federal advisory committee that directed the 2014 National Climate Assessment and was a lead author of its Southeast and Caribbean chapter. He co-authored NOAA’s sea level rise scenario reports for the United States (2012, 2017 and 2022) and the Department of Defense regional sea level scenarios, and has served on National Research Council and National Academies committees on the Klamath River, the California Bay-Delta, the Edwards Aquifer and modernizing Probable Maximum Precipitation. He is a member of the California Bay-Delta Independent Science Board.

His paper with Jose Salas, Revisiting the Concepts of Return Period and Risk for Nonstationary Hydrologic Extreme Events, received the 2015 Norman Medal of the American Society of Civil Engineers. He holds a B.Sc. (Hons.) in Civil Engineering from the University of Ceylon, Peradeniya, an M.Eng. in Hydrology from the University of Roorkee, India, and a Ph.D. in Civil Engineering (Hydrology and Water Resources) from Colorado State University, where he was also on the faculty. He is a registered Professional Engineer in Florida, a Diplomate Water Resources Engineer and a Fellow of ASCE’s Environmental & Water Resources Institute.
